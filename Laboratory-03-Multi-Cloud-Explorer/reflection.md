# Checkpoint 8 – Reflection

## Reflection

Throughout this laboratory activity, I learned more about the three major cloud platforms: Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP). Among the three, AWS impressed me the most because of its wide range of cloud services and its ability to support different types of workloads, from simple applications to large enterprise systems.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three provide computing, storage, networking, database, security, and identity management services. They also support virtualization, containerization, automation, scalability, and Linux-based workloads. Although their services have different names and features, they provide similar solutions for common cloud computing requirements.

Business requirements are important when selecting a cloud platform because every organization has different needs, budgets, technologies, and goals. For example, a company that already uses Microsoft products may benefit more from Azure, while an organization focused on artificial intelligence and Kubernetes may consider GCP. A startup with a limited budget may choose a platform based on cost, scalability, and available services.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant helped me understand that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. It requires analyzing the organization's requirements and matching them with the appropriate cloud services. I learned that factors such as cost, performance, scalability, compatibility, security, and business goals should be considered before making a recommendation.

This laboratory also improved my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio because I was able to organize my research, comparisons, recommendations, and practical Linux investigation in one repository. The activity helped me develop both technical knowledge and decision-making skills that are useful when designing cloud solutions.
